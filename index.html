The questions you actually ask, answered from your plan

Retirement Readiness turns the projection into plain answers, each worked out from your own numbers rather than a rule of thumb:

  • If you walked away today, could the portfolio carry this lifestyle?
  • What would working longer actually buy you?
  • Where does your first five years of money come from, and how does it reach you?
  • If the market fell 20% right after you retire, could you stay retired without changing your lifestyle?
  • Are your biggest expenses landing in the years that can least absorb them?

Built so we can’t sell what we never hold

Your numbers are your business. We don't collect your plan data, we don't have user accounts, and we never sell your financial profile.

  • No user accounts to run a projection
  • No bank linking required to start
  • Plan data + math on your device (free and Premium)
  • No third-party beacons by default — anonymous visit analytics are opt-in and off until you turn them on
  • Backup you own — export a file to your disk; optional encrypted backup

Loading the app still contacts our host to serve the page. Optional Live Pricing can send ticker symbols only to a spreadsheet you control — never share counts or dollars invested. We don’t claim the browser “never touches the network.”

Different job than sync-first tools

Many “free” planners start by connecting banks so balances live on their servers — then retention, coaching, or managed money. Others skip linking but still require an account and keep your plan in the cloud.

OutlastRetirement is a local planning instrument: see if your current path outlasts you, without uploading your life or getting walked to an advisor.

Free core. Optional depth.

Free — OutlastRetirement

  • Monte Carlo — 1,000 simulated markets and one verdict: will it last?
  • Every real retirement since 1928 — your plan replayed through each start year, plus the crashes of 1929 to 2008
  • Retirement Readiness — a plain answer to every question, from walking away today to a 20% market fall
  • Optimizers — Social Security claiming, Roth conversion ladders and withdrawal order
  • The full plan — federal and state tax, IRMAA, ACA credits, RMDs, Year by Year detail, scenarios and spending guardrails
