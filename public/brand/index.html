Each mark is keyed to transparency, so pairing one with the wrong ground makes it vanish rather than sit in a colored box. That is deliberate: a wrong pairing should be obvious.

Color

  • --or-navy#0F2744 · dark surfaces, ink on cream
  • --or-cream#FFFAF4 · light surfaces
  • --or-paper#EAE6DD · type and marks on navy
  • --or-teal#2A7F7A · path and arrow

The supplied rasters sample a hair off the token — about #356D72 on the full path. The token wins, and there is only ever one teal.

Rules

  • Paths and arrows never end on a downslope. Both strokes finish up and to the right.
  • No round end-dot — it reads as a resting point. The upper stroke of the full mark ends in a full arrowhead.
  • Full mark: two teal strokes to the left of the stacked type, exactly one arrowhead. The lower stroke has no arrow and no dot.
  • Short mark: the arrow cuts through the R. It never parks beside the letters.
  • Never stretch, outline, drop-shadow, recolour the arrow on its own, or add a second arrowhead.
  • Never redraw the wordmark in live type — it stays in the asset. UI chrome may use a clean sans.
  • No competitor name appears on a mark or in a lockup.
  • Clearspace around a mark is about 0.6 × its height.
