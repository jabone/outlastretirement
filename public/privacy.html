Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

Everything you enter into OutlastRetirement — your ages, balances, income, and spending assumptions — stays on your device. The App never transmits it; it leaves only when you move it yourself, as described under "Moving Your Data Off Your Device." Emailing us is the one place we hold anything about you, and it is never connected to your plan. Optional anonymous usage analytics are off by default and only run if you turn them on; see "Anonymous Usage Analytics" below for exactly what that includes.

Overview

OutlastRetirement ("the App") is an educational retirement cash flow calculator designed to help individuals model their financial future. We are committed to your privacy. This policy describes how the App handles information, including the limited, anonymous analytics data described below.

Data We Do Not Collect

OutlastRetirement does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal or financial planning information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information through the App. You can type names into your plan, such as a spouse, heirs, account labels, Life Plan notes or the short notes you write about a plan (“Your three answers”), but they stay on your device and are never transmitted. If you email us, we of course have your address; see If You Email Us below.
  • Financial account numbers, balances, or transaction data
  • Any of the retirement-plan inputs or results you enter into the App (ages, income, savings, spending, tax settings, simulation outputs, etc.)
  • Social Security numbers or government identifiers
  • Precise location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Individually identifiable usage or behavioral data — see below for the limited, anonymous analytics we do use
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data sent to us

How Your Data Is Stored

All information you enter into OutlastRetirement — including retirement ages, account balances, income figures, and spending assumptions — is stored exclusively on your device using the browser's local storage. This data:

  • Is never sent anywhere by the App
  • Is not backed up to any server operated by OutlastRetirement — there is no such server
  • Is not accessible to the developer, because we never receive a copy of it
  • Can be erased at any time with Erase everything on this device in Preferences → Your Data, which removes everything the App has stored in this browser once you confirm, or by clearing this site's data in your browser. Reset Plan to Defaults is different: it resets only the plan you have open and erases nothing else.

Moving Your Data Off Your Device

The App never moves your plan anywhere. You can, and these are the ways you can move it — each one something you do deliberately, none of them automatic, and none of them routed through us:

  • Backup Everything downloads your plan as a file to your computer. What happens to that file afterwards is entirely up to you: where you keep it, whether you email it, whether you encrypt it. You can password-protect it on the way out if you want to.
  • Save to a cloud folder writes that same file to a location you pick — typically a folder your own Dropbox, Google Drive, iCloud or OneDrive client already watches. Worth being precise about how this works: the App has no account with any of those providers, no sign-in, and no API key. It writes an ordinary file to a folder you chose; your own sync software is what carries it to the cloud, under whatever terms you already have with that provider. We are not in the path and never see it.
  • Print / Save as PDF sends a report to your browser’s print dialog. Where it goes from there, a printer or a PDF file, is your choice.
  • CSV exports, such as the Year by Year table or your holdings, download a spreadsheet file to your computer, the same way a backup does.
  • Live Pricing (optional, off until you connect it) is the only feature that sends anything out while you use the App. It sends a bare ticker symbol — for example VTI — to a Google Sheet in your own Google account, and reads back a price. The Sheet’s script then looks the symbol up with Google Finance and Yahoo Finance from Google’s servers; those services see the symbol, not you. It never sends how many shares you own, which accounts hold them, what they cost you, or any dollar amount. The Sheet is yours; we have no access to it.

If you would rather none of that were possible, turning on Local-Only Mode (Preferences → Your Data) stops the App’s optional connections, Live Pricing and analytics included. Your browser still loads the App and checks for updates when you reload. It is enforced in the code at the point every request would be made, not by hiding buttons.

Premium Modules

The code for the Premium features — Detailed Holdings, Retirement Spending, Readiness in Full and Relocation & Residency — is kept separate from the rest of the App. It is not part of what your browser downloads from outlastretirement.com; it is fetched, one module at a time, from our own module server, premium.outlastretirement.com, and only when a valid Premium Access key is present and the first time you open the part of the App that uses it. The Demo Plan is the one exception: it shows a read-only Detailed Holdings view to visitors who have no key, so while Demo Mode is on and no valid key is present the App sends the module server a demo delivery key that is built into the App itself. It is the same for every visitor, it is not a Premium key (pasting it into Preferences unlocks nothing), it carries no address and nothing about you, and the server will hand it only the Detailed Holdings module. A Premium user in the demo uses their own key, as everywhere else.

Here is exactly what happens, and the code enforcing it is in the App’s published source:

  • The App sends the module server one thing: your Premium Access key, so the server can check it is valid and unexpired. It is sent as a request header, never in a web address. The key carries the email address it was issued to (see "If You Email Us" below), so that address is inside what the server receives; the server checks the key's signature and expiry and keeps no record of its own; Vercel, which hosts it, keeps the same short-lived request logs it keeps for every page (below). Nothing from your plan or your holdings is sent — the server delivers code to your browser; your data never travels the other way.
  • The server answers with a signed module. The App verifies the signature against a public key built into the App before running a single line of it, so what you run is what we published, not something altered on the way.
  • A module may later fetch a few supporting files from the same server without a key: third-party code it loads only when needed, such as the PDF reader that Detailed Holdings uses when you import a brokerage statement, the chart library, and the Live Pricing script you copy into Google Sheets. None of it is Premium code. The files are not signed themselves, but since October 19, 2026 the signed module carries a fingerprint of each one and the App runs a file only if it matches, so an altered file is refused. They are fetched the way any web page fetches a file, so the server (and Vercel’s request logs, below) sees that a browser asked for that file and nothing else — not your key, and never the statement or anything in it, which is read entirely on your device.
  • Premium modules run under the same rules as the rest of the App. They do not store anything outside the App’s own data on your device, and they make no network connections of their own — with the one exception already described above, Live Pricing to your own Google Sheet, which only the Detailed Holdings module uses and only when you have connected it.
  • Local-Only Mode stops this connection too. While it is on, no Premium module is fetched, and a Premium part of the App that has not been loaded yet shows a note saying so instead. A module that was already loaded in the current session keeps working: once Detailed Holdings, Retirement Spending, Readiness in Full and Relocation & Residency have each been opened once, you can turn Local-Only Mode on and carry on using all four with no further connection until you reload the page.
  • If your key has lapsed, Premium parts of the App lock, but your holdings and everything else you entered stay on your device, readable and restorable from a backup, exactly as before.

Which version of each Premium module is loaded is shown in Preferences. The App itself is open source (github.com/jabone/outlastretirement, under the GNU Affero General Public License); the Premium modules are a separate work and are not.

What Our Host Sees

The App’s pages and code are served by our hosting provider, Vercel Inc. When your browser asks for a page, Vercel’s servers receive your IP address and browser type, as any web server does, and keep short-lived request logs (the address asked for, the response, and in some views the IP address) that we can see in Vercel’s dashboard for a short time. We don’t export, analyze or keep them, we send them nowhere else, and nothing you type into the App is part of those requests.

Anonymous Usage Analytics

These analytics are off by default. The App sends no usage data at all unless you turn on "Anonymous Usage Analytics" in Preferences → Your Data. If you never turn it on, this whole section does not apply to you and nothing described here is ever sent.

When you switch it on, the App uses Vercel Web Analytics, a privacy-focused service from our hosting provider, Vercel Inc., to understand overall traffic. It then sends the following anonymous, aggregate technical information to Vercel's servers on each visit:

  • Which part of the App was opened, as the tab name only (for example, Scenarios): no sub-page, no search or link parameters, and nothing after the address
  • The site you came from, if any. That site decides how much of its address your browser passes on; most, including search engines, pass on only their domain. Moving between our own pages passes on only our domain.
  • Approximate location (country/region, derived from network address — not precise GPS location)
  • Device type, operating system, and browser (e.g. "Mobile, iOS, Safari")
  • Timestamp of the visit

This is the same category of information a web server logs by default — it does not include anything you type into the App. None of your names, ages, balances, income, spending, tax settings, or simulation results are ever included in this data, because the App never sends that information anywhere (see "How Your Data Is Stored" above).

The service uses no cookies or persistent device identifiers and cannot identify you individually or link your visits across other apps or websites. Visitors are distinguished only by a one-way hash of the incoming request, which is automatically discarded after 24 hours. For Vercel's own technical documentation on exactly what is collected, see their Web Analytics privacy policy. Turning the setting back off stops all of it immediately, and turning on the App's Local-Only Mode overrides the setting entirely — while that is on, no analytics are sent.

The App's fonts are bundled into the App itself rather than loaded from a third party, so opening the App reveals nothing to any font host. OutlastRetirement does not use any advertising networks, third-party trackers, or data-sharing services beyond the optional Web Analytics described above.

If You Email Us

Premium access is arranged by email — today it is invite-only, and if that changes we will say so here — so emailing us is the one point where we hold something about you. We think you should know exactly what that is.

  • What we have: your email address, and whatever you choose to write to us. Nothing more — we do not ask for your name, and there is no form that collects anything else. If we send you a Premium key, we also keep a one-line record of it — the address, the key’s serial number and its expiry date — in a file on our own computer, not on any server, so that a key can be canceled or replaced if you ask.
  • Where it sits: a Google Workspace mailbox for outlastretirement.com. Google hosts it as our email provider, under its Google Workspace terms. Mail sent to our earlier address, outlastretirement@gmail.com, is forwarded there, and anything sent to that address before October 2026 stays in that Gmail mailbox, which Google hosts under its own terms.
  • How long: indefinitely. We keep correspondence in the mailbox and do not delete it on a schedule. Ask us to delete yours and we will.
  • What we use it for: replying to you, and sending Premium enable instructions. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not sell, rent, or share your address.

It is never linked to your plan. This is not a promise about our intentions — it is how the App is built. The App never sends us your plan data, so we do not have it to link anything to. The Premium access key we send you carries four things: the email address we sent it to, the fact that Premium is enabled, when it expires, and (from October 18, 2026) a random serial number, so that one key can be canceled without canceling anyone else’s. Nothing else — no name, no device, no plan data. The address is there so that a key says whose it is: the App shows it under Preferences › Premium Access, and a key that turns up where it should not can be traced to the address it was issued to. The key is checked by the Premium module server when a module is fetched (see "Premium Modules" above), so that server sees the address inside your key on each fetch, and nothing else about you; it keeps no record of those fetches. Nothing in the App sends us anything, so we still cannot tell who is using it or how.

Payment: Premium is free while it is invite-only. There is no checkout, no price, and no payment processor, so we hold no payment or card information whatsoever. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before any payment is taken, not after.

Your Choices

Your plan data is yours in the most literal sense: it is on your device, and you can export it with Backup Everything, or erase it with Erase everything on this device, at any time from Preferences → Your Data. We have no copy to hand over or delete.

For anything you have emailed us, write to the address at the bottom of this page and we will tell you what correspondence we hold from you, or delete it. We will not ask you to prove anything beyond replying from the address in question.

No Account Required

Children's Privacy
